# Social media plan: Renovation OS

**Goal:** people who are about to renovate, or are in the middle of it, see you as the person who knows how to keep a renovation under control. Then they click through to Etsy or Gumroad.

**Rule of thumb:** 80% useful tips and stories, 20% product. Nobody follows an advert, but people follow someone who saves them money.

## Where to post, in order of priority

| Platform | Why | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pinterest** | Best traffic source for Etsy templates. Pins keep working for months, and people search "renovation budget", "kitchen remodel cost" | 1000×1500 pins: tip + screenshot, linking to the listing | Low, batch 20 pins in an afternoon |
| **Instagram** (Reels + carousels) | Home and renovation content is huge there; carousels are great for "5 mistakes…" | Reels of 15–30 s screen recordings; 5–8 slide carousels | Medium |
| **TikTok** | Fastest way to reach new people with zero followers | The same short videos as Reels | Medium, reuse the Reels |
| **Facebook groups** | Renovation, self-build and first-home groups have buyers *right now* | Helpful answers and posts. No ads; mention the tool only when relevant or asked | Low |
| **Reddit** | r/HomeImprovement, r/DIY, r/Renovations, r/FirstTimeHomeBuyer, local subreddits | Genuinely useful posts. Self-promotion rules are strict, so read each sub's rules | Low, high value |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Free extra reach for the same videos | Upload your Reels | Very low |

Skip LinkedIn and X for this product; homeowners don't shop there.

Instagram and Facebook both work. Run them from one Meta Business account and cross-post Reels to both. Facebook's value is mostly in the **groups**, not your own page.

## Simple weekly rhythm

- **Monday:** 1 Reel/TikTok (screen recording + voice-over or text captions).
- **Wednesday:** 1 carousel (a tip).
- **Friday:** 5 Pinterest pins + 2 helpful answers in Facebook groups or Reddit.
- **Every day, 10 minutes:** reply to comments and messages. That is where sales start.

## Video recipe (no face needed)

1. Screen-record the DEMO file (OBS, Mac's Cmd+Shift+5, or Windows Game Bar).
2. Hook in the first 2 seconds as big text: "Your cheapest quote might be the most expensive one 👇".
3. Show 3 moments, 3–5 seconds each.
4. End card: "Renovation OS — link in bio".
5. Trending audio at low volume, or a calm voice-over.

## Links and bio

- **Bio:** "Renovation planner spreadsheets 🏠 Stay on budget & on time. Made for Excel." Link in bio → your Etsy shop or Gumroad page. Use Linktree if you want both.
- Add a **UTM-tagged link** or a platform-specific discount code (e.g. `INSTA15`, `PIN15`) so you know which platform actually sells.

## What to avoid

- Posting the same promo link in groups: you will be banned. Be helpful first.
- Claims you can't back up ("saves €10,000"). Use real examples such as the demo numbers.
- Showing buyers' private data. Only use the demo file.
