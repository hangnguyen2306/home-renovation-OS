# Posting the 18 pins on Pinterest

**Before you start:** open `marketing/make_pins.py`, set `LINK = "https://YOUR-GUMROAD-LINK"` to your real product URL, and run `python3 marketing/make_pins.py`. That refreshes `pins.csv`. Or just paste the link by hand in step 3 below.

## One-time setup
1. Create a free **Pinterest business account** (or convert your personal one). It gives you analytics and scheduling.
2. **Claim your website** if you have one. Optional.
3. Create 3 **boards**: *Renovation Planning*, *Renovation Budget*, *Renovation Tips*. These are the board names used in `pins.csv`.

## For each pin (about 3 minutes each)
1. **Create ▸ Create Pin** and upload the image, e.g. `01_budget_template.png`.
2. **Title** and **Description**: copy them from `pins.csv`.
3. **Link**: your Gumroad product URL. Use the same link for every pin.
4. **Board**: the one in `pins.csv`.
5. **Alt text**: from `pins.csv`. It helps accessibility and search.
6. **Tagged topics**: add 3–5 of the keywords.
7. Choose **Publish at a later date** and pick the date: Day 1 = tomorrow, Day 2 = the day after, and so on. Around 8–9 pm local time works well.
8. **Schedule**, then repeat for the next pin.

Pinterest publishes each pin automatically on its date. You don't need to be online.

## Afterwards
- After ~3 weeks, check **Analytics**: which pins get the most *outbound clicks*? Make 3–5 new variations of the winners (new headline, same topic).
- Re-pin the best pins to a second board a month later.
- Keep adding 3–5 new pins per week. Pinterest rewards fresh images.
